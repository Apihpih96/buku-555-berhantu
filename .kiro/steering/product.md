# Product: Buku 555 Berhantu

Malaysian market traders (pasar ikan, pasar pagi, kedai runcit) have tracked customer credit ("hutang") for decades in the iconic red-and-blue Buku 555 notebook. Paper gets wet at the fish stall, pages get lost, and asking a neighbour for money is awkward.

Buku 555 Berhantu resurrects the Buku 555 as a phone-first web app:
- The trader speaks or types an entry in Bahasa Melayu (e.g. "Kak Ros ambil 2 kilo kembung RM18, bayar Jumaat").
- It parses customer, item, amount (RM) and due day into a ledger entry.
- It shows who owes what, total outstanding, and who is overdue.
- For overdue customers, a polite "hantu" (friendly ghost) reminder message is prepared and opened in WhatsApp via a wa.me link. The trader always presses send; nothing is sent automatically.
- Marking an entry paid "exorcises" it (the ghost leaves).

Primary user: a 60-year-old fish seller using a cheap Android phone at 5am. So: big tap targets, Malay-first UI (English toggle), works offline, no login, no server.

Halloween theme: haunted old notebook — aged lined paper, faded ink, the 555 cover, a small ghost. Spooky but warm and readable, never scary for the user.
